# Decisions

## Keep the frozen selected player during portfolio preparation

The standalone submission embeds the selected runtime sources. Rather than change the search while cleaning up its presentation, this package retains those exact bytes and places new documentation and evaluation utilities beside them. Hash and embedded-source checks make accidental drift visible.

The 12 historical paired checks compared equal-work calls between the standalone package and frozen reference. The current lightweight tests check identity and specific behaviors; they do not replace those historical checks or create new performance evidence.

## Select for the tested submission conditions and preserve counterevidence

VerifiedDenial08 won 14 of 22 eligible internal games in the final selection evidence. That was the basis for selecting it under the tested submission clock. Earlier evidence was mixed: the after-action report records a Repair10 lead of 7–3 at both 5- and 15-second clocks, and a Revision30 advantage in matched historical common-opponent comparisons. Different boards and execution conditions prevent attributing the later reversal solely to extra thinking time.

The schedule changed in response to observed outcomes and players had possible prior exposure to layouts. The repository therefore reports the sample and its limitations, rather than claiming an independently measured general advantage. Larger books, deeper search, and parallel work were not treated as automatic improvements.

One recorded frontier-verification candidate gained two wins but lost two baseline winning controls, leaving both candidates at four wins in six checks. It was rejected. Correct leaf facts had not certified the root choice because the branches were checked unevenly. This illustrates why failure cases and preserved wins matter alongside aggregate totals.
